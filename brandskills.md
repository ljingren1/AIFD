# MATIN KIM — OFF THE CLOCK
## Brand Skills for AI Image Generation

> Unofficial student concept for a fictional Spring 2027 NYC-exclusive Matin Kim baseball cap collection. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Matin Kim.

### 1. Brand purpose
OFF THE CLOCK captures the moments between obligations: the coffee run, the subway ride, the long walk home. The hat is the visual anchor of every image. Tagline: **No plans. Just presence.** Audience: fashion-conscious adults 18–30 who appreciate Korean streetwear and downtown New York style.

### 2. Product identity — consistent in all images
A minimalist, softly structured six-panel cotton baseball cap with a curved brim, washed finish, subtle stitching, small clean cream or tonal embroidery reading “Matin Kim” (only if the model can render lettering accurately). Do not invent any additional brands, badges, slogans, or cap silhouettes. Four fictional colorways:
- **After Hours**: washed charcoal black, approximate #232323, cream embroidery.
- **Slow Morning**: vintage cream/beige, approximate #EAE2D3, tonal embroidery.
- **Downtown Blue**: faded navy, approximate #68758A, light embroidery.
- **Last Train**: deep burgundy, approximate #6E333D, cream embroidery.
If the AI model struggles to reproduce the logo accurately, generate a clean blank embroidered area and place final lettering with HTML/CSS or professional editing.

### 3. Visual palette
Charcoal #232323 (primary type and shadows); vintage cream #EAE2D3 (negative space); faded navy #68758A; burgundy #6E333D (sparingly); concrete stone #9C998D. Approximate usage: 60% charcoal/cream, 30% navy/stone, 10% burgundy. Slight desaturation. No neon, glossy gradients, or candy colors.

### 4. Type and graphic system
Cormorant Garamond for display headlines and italic poetic statements. Inter for navigation, labels, dates, and body copy. Headlines are sparse, editorial, spacious and legible. The camera image should leave intentional clean negative space for typography. Prefer generating **photography without text**, then overlay accurate type in the webpage. Logo direction: a minimal open clock face crossed by a curved cap-brim gesture; do not claim this is the brand's official logo.

### 5. Photography and lighting
Photorealistic elevated fashion-editorial image, credible human proportions, subtle 35mm-film grain, textured cotton, nuanced natural skin, a hint of film halation. Prefer soft overcast NYC daylight, gentle morning side light, or authentic restrained nighttime direct flash. Candid motion and unposed attitude over glossy commercial posing. Avoid exaggerated blur over the cap, plastic skin, CGI sheen, hyper-sharpening, excessive bokeh and generic stock-photo smiles.

### 6. Framing and camera vocabulary
- Product hero: three-quarter front view, cap crisp, softly textured background, shadow beneath brim.
- Lifestyle: natural adult model in frame, cap clearly readable, documentary streetwear styling.
- Detail: close-up macro of fabric, brim stitching, closure, or small embroidery; real textile scale.
- Environmental: authentic NYC setting, cap still visually central; clean space for campaign copy.
- Flat lay: deliberately composed cap with understated everyday objects; overhead 90° angle.
Available angles include front-on, three-quarter, side profile, overhead, macro, street-level wide, and low-angle editorial. Do not crop away the product accidentally.

### 7. Authentic NYC settings
SoHo cast-iron facades, Lower East Side worn brick, Brooklyn neighborhood sidewalk, café window, subway staircase, station platform, rooftop skyline. Depict plausible contemporary New York architecture and street detail without invented transit signs or unreadable logos. Avoid Times Square neon overload and cliché tourist views unless explicitly needed.

### 8. Styling
Relaxed denim, charcoal oversized jacket, simple white tee, soft knitwear, minimal jewelry, practical bag, quiet monochrome layers. Natural hair and movement. The cap is the product, not a background accessory. Represent adult models and authentic everyday activities; avoid celebrity likenesses.

### 9. Tone and copy
Poetic, minimal, slightly independent. Main campaign: **OFF THE CLOCK**. Core line: **No plans. Just presence.** Secondary lines: “Take your time.” “Last train. First impression.” “Made for the in-between.” “Your day. Your pace.” If text is needed, reserve negative space for accurate typography added later.

### 10. Platform and composition rules
- Billboard: 16:9 / 1920×1080; monumental simple read, wide negative space.
- Instagram feed: 4:5 / 1080×1350; strong centered or offset product silhouette.
- Instagram reel/story: 9:16 / 1080×1920; safe central framing and vertical margins.
- NYC subway poster: 2:3 / 1200×1800; bold single focal point, high legibility.
- Magazine spread: 3:2 / 1800×1200; editorial wide composition.
- Square social: 1:1 / 1080×1080; concise product image.
- Website hero: 21:9 / 2100×900; generous edge space, focal point on right or center.
Always choose the requested composition at generation time instead of cropping a different ratio after.

### 11. Mandatory prompt elements
Every prompt needs: campaign name; fictional product and colorway; selected subject/image type; specific angle and lighting; NYC location or deliberate studio setting; clothing/styling as needed; exact aspect ratio and pixel dimensions; campaign colors; 35mm editorial treatment; negative-space requirement; exclusions; and instruction to avoid generating unreliable typography.

### 12. Off-brand checklist
Reject images with: neon colors, futuristic sci-fi, luxury-glam makeup, polished product CGI, extra logo marks, misspelled brand text, non-baseball hats, exaggerated streetwear logos, ornate accessories, tourist souvenir visuals, too much saturation, fake NYC landmarks, hats hidden by hair, and inconsistent brim shapes.

### 13. Quality review
Verify: 1) is the hat prominent? 2) colorway correct? 3) fabric/embroidery believable? 4) NYC context authentic when requested? 5) requested ratio correct? 6) type-safe area exists? 7) muted editorial mood consistent? 8) no stray AI text or logos? If anything fails, refine prompt and regenerate.

### 14. AI-use disclosure
All proposed campaign visuals and tools are student-directed AI explorations for an unofficial concept, not Matin Kim marketing material. Source imagery and actual brand marks used as inspiration must be credited and handled appropriately.

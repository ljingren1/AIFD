ART DIRECTION / 06

The rules.

A small set of creative constraints keeps campaign photography, layouts and AI-generated work recognizably on brand.

01 / CAMERA

Unposed,
not undone.

Candid walking shots, direct flash after dark, off-center framing, cropped portraits and close-ups of stitching. Allow some blur and fine film grain; preserve the hat as the focal point.

02 / LOCATION

The city
is a texture.

SoHo cast-iron façades, subway entrances, Lower East Side poster walls and neighborhood cafés. NYC is a lived-in backdrop rather than a tourist postcard.

03 / GRAPHICS

Less said,
more felt.

One clear message per visual. Oversized serif typography, restrained sans-serif annotations, cream negative space and no neon colors, busy gradients or unnecessary effects.

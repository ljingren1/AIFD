03 · Design a Chair

Iterative Prompt Progression & In-Painting Refinement

A step-by-step documentation of AI image generation. Beginning from a simple baseline prompt, each subsequent iteration layers environmental context, lighting, textural medium, camera perspective, spatial markups, and comprehensive architectural styling.

Stage 01 Baseline Prompt

Solitary Chair in Room

Prompt
"A chair in the middle of a room. Generate it."
Stage 02 Context Added

Material Specification & Cafe Environment

New Directives Added
+ Specify chair material: "A white chair with cushioning"
+ New setting: "in a cafe."
Stage 03 Atmosphere & Lighting

Midday Light & Architectural Elements

New Directives Added
+ Lighting & Time: "in the middle of the day. Sunlight shining through the window."
+ Interior palette: "minimalistic look with light grey cement walls, indoor plants, and modern minimal lighting."
Stage 04 Stylistic Medium

Detailed Canvas Sketch Aesthetic

New Directives Added
+ Art Medium: "Generate it where it looks sketched out on a canvas, appearing very detailed with fine architectural linework."
Stage 05 Perspective Framing

Front-Centered View & Extended Background

New Directives Added
+ Camera position: "Shot from the front of the chair where it shows the background of the cafe more, while keeping the chair centered in the frame."
Stage 06 (Input) Copilot In-Painting Markup

Visual Markup for In-Painting Edits

Markup Instructions
■ Red square by plant: Designate location for self-help station.
✕ Crossed circle: Mark tabletop vase/plant for removal.
▭ Floor rectangle: Outline placement for woven area rug.
Stage 07 Copilot Refinement Result

Edited Generation from Image Markup

Editing Prompt to Copilot
"Add a self-help station with straws and napkins by the red square by the plant. Remove the plant where circled with an 'X'. Add a woven rug where the rectangle is drawn on the floor."
Final Masterwork Gemini Comprehensive Prompt

Architectural Watercolor Sketchbook Rendering

Refined Comprehensive Prompt
"Front-facing white cushioned cafe chair centered in frame, detailed hand-drawn sketch on textured canvas, architectural pencil and ink illustration style, extremely detailed linework, subtle watercolor washes, bright midday sunlight streaming through floor-to-ceiling windows, soft shadows across polished concrete floor, minimalist cafe interior, espresso bar and pastry display, modern track lighting, natural wood furniture, self-service station with organized straws and napkins beside a tall potted plant, rectangular woven neutral-toned rug beneath chair, Scandinavian aesthetic, realistic perspective, sketchbook rendering."

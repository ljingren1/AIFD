02 / Consistency above all
Creative brief
OFF THE CLOCK is a fictional Spring 2027 NYC launch of everyday Matin Kim-inspired caps. The campaign follows ordinary moments outside schedules and celebrates relaxed individuality. Images must feel candid, urban, slightly nostalgic, and unmistakably part of the same collection.
Headline typography: Cormorant Garamond
Labels / interface: Inter
On brand / off brand
  • DO: natural light, film grain, understated adult streetwear, authentic NYC settings.
  • DO: emphasize cap construction, stitching and muted colorway.
  • DON'T: neon, glam makeup, polished 3D renders, fake city landmarks.
  • DON'T: random lettering, extra brands, distorted embroidery, hidden product.

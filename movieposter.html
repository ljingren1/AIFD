Movie Poster Design Iterations

Generative Exploration & Alignment Workflow
Reference Visual

Original Concept Sketch

Primary layout blueprint defining the vertical tension: a submerged feline predator with its mouth open displaying fangs at the lower boundary, oriented upward toward a worried cartoon fish and a descending fishing hook at the water's surface.

Iteration Sequence
Poster 1 Prompt

"can you make me a new movie poster. the cat at the bottom will have its fangs out with it's mouth open. it will be in a body of water and there will be a cartoon worried fish at the top. there will be a fishing pole coming after the fish too."

Poster 2 Prompt

"can you make the artstyle more cohesive. make the cat underwater instead of submerging from the water. make the title more cat or fish themed. make the colors more bright."

Poster 3 Prompt

"make the poster more realistic. keep the style of the first poster."

Poster 4 Prompt

"can you make the movie poster only have the title without the extra words. I want the cat and the fish to look realistic. remove the caption from the fish. make it minimalistic."

Poster 5 Prompt

"can you make it so the cat is fully submerged into water instead of it's head above the water. keep everything else the same."
